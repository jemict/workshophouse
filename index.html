Specialist Vocational Maker Studio (Redditch)

Hands-on creativity. Calm spaces. Real autonomy.

A welcoming, low-arousal daytime studio for young people aged 9 to 16 navigating neurodivergence, sensory sensitivities, and Emotionally Based School Avoidance (EBSA). We balance practical digital layout and creative printmaking with soothing, hands-on maker crafts, including air-dry clay, textiles, paper arts, pipe cleaner sculpture, and cold cake decorating tools, all supported by portfolio-based AQA unit awards.

Clear Educational Safeguards & Commissioning Ethics

Strictly Educational, Never Commercial Labour: Workshop House exists solely to provide therapeutic, vocational, and creative education. Young people are students on an educational journey, never a workforce. Every session is designed for personal development, sensory regulation, and accredited learning.

Structured Learning Materials: Learners regularly complete simulated customer briefs, practice design exercises, and structured curriculum worksheets mapped to their portfolios. When learners choose to experience real-world maker tasks through our community brand, Dotty Moo, participation is voluntary, unhurried, and supported by a guaranteed adult backstop. Commercial output deadlines and store operations are handled entirely by adult staff outside teaching hours, ensuring zero production pressure on young people.

Asset-Locked Community Focus: 100% of trading surplus from social enterprise products remains strictly locked within our Community Interest Company to fund learner equipment, workshop blanks, and community bursaries.
