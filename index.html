Specialist Non-School Maker Studio • Redditch

Hands-on enterprise. Sensory-safe design. Real autonomy.

A low-arousal daytime studio for young people aged 9–16 navigating neurodivergence, sensory sensitivities, and Emotionally Based School Avoidance (EBSA). Practical digital design and dye-sublimation making with accredited AQA awards.

Explore Pathways Arrange a Quiet Visit

The Core Approach: "Enterprise as an Anchor"

Removing the institutional pressure of bells, uniforms, and rigid hierarchies—replacing them with tangible craftsmanship and genuine workplace purpose.

🏠

De-Institutionalised Setting

A quiet studio layout instead of rows of desks. Young people are welcomed as valued workshop team members, shifting the dynamic from pupil compliance to mutual respect.

💡

Micro-Groups & High Ratios

Capped at 6 learners per session with a strict 1:3 staff ratio. Led directly by a Qualified Teacher (QTS) with over 20 years of specialist SEND and computing experience.

🖨️

Authentic Real-World Making

Learners master graphic design in Canva, vector scaling, dye-sublimation print presses, and dispatch logistics. Real tasks with real stakes that build organic dopamine and self-worth.

Powered by Live Social Enterprise

Our practical curriculum is anchored in the live manufacturing operations of Dotty Moo, an active commercial personalisation brand. Learners never complete abstract simulated worksheets; they make real products for real orders. 100% of trading revenue is locked directly within the Community Interest Company to subsidise community bursaries and maker equipment.
