Educational Pathways & Studio Sessions

Purposeful, low-arousal vocational learning focused on real-world creative skills, confidence, and autonomy. Assessed through relaxed evidence portfolios and accredited AQA unit awards rather than exam pressure, with individual progress mapped to EHCP outcomes and Preparation for Adulthood targets where applicable.

Please note: Workshop House is a specialist, part-time vocational maker studio. We are not a school and do not provide full-time school registrations.

Wellbeing & Regulation First

We recognise that many young people joining us have experienced placement breakdowns, sensory exhaustion, and severe school-related anxiety. A young person cannot create, design, or learn while their nervous system is in fight-or-flight.

Our priority is repairing trust, self-worth, and emotional regulation through low-demand, trauma-informed pacing. Every session incorporates soft landings, predictable visual schedules, and calm breakout spaces where learners can regulate without pressure.

Commissioned / Local Authority & Schools

Statutory Placements (Section 19 & Section 61)

Bespoke part-time daytime placements for young people where conventional full-time school attendance is compromised by medical needs, severe anxiety, or neurodivergent burnout.

  • Section 19 Alternative Provision: Commissioned by mainstream schools and inclusion teams for dual-rolled pupils requiring low-arousal re-engagement and vocational skills.
  • Section 61 EOTAS Packages: Funded via Local Authority SEND teams or parent-held EHCP Personal Budgets where conventional education is deemed unsuitable.
  • Accredited Portfolio Progress: Evidence mapped directly to EHCP Section F outcomes and AQA Unit Award Scheme certificates without exam pressure.
  • Flexible Sessional Delivery: Arranged across morning (10:00 to 12:00) or afternoon (13:00 to 15:00) sessions, capped strictly under 15 hours weekly per learner.
Community / Parent Direct

Elective Home Education (EHE) Sessions

A gentle, pressure-free creative community hub for home-educated young people needing supportive peer connection, sensory regulation, and self-directed project autonomy.

  • Low-Demand Tactile Making: Exploring sensory-safe clay modelling, textiles, paper craft, cold decorating tools, digital design, and sublimation printing.
  • Supportive Micro-Cohorts: Capped strictly at 6 learners per session with high staff ratios (1:3), prioritising nervous system safety and genuine self-pacing.
  • AQA Unit Award Scheme: Optional non-exam accreditation celebrating practical skills and creative portfolios with certificates included at zero extra cost.
  • Flexible Attendance Options: Attended weekly in calm 2-hour morning or afternoon slots, with gentle door-open taster sessions before starting.

Placement Suitability & Learner Profile

To ensure the studio remains a calm, physically safe environment for all learners, we maintain clear admissions criteria:

🌱

Who We Support

Ages 9 to 16 experiencing Emotionally Based School Avoidance (EBSA), neurodivergence (Autism, ADHD, and PDA profiles), sensory differences, and those thriving outside conventional classrooms.

🤝

Micro-Group Baselines

Delivered in micro-cohorts (maximum 6 learners) at a 1:3 staff ratio. Learners must be able to share a calm studio environment safely alongside peers without requiring continuous, restrictive 1:1 intervention.

🛡️

Safety Boundaries

Because the studio operates heat presses and craft tools, we cannot accommodate young people displaying active physical violence, severe property destruction, or unmanaged sexually harmful behaviours.

Session Timetable & Workshop Flow

The studio runs two distinct 2-hour delivery blocks on Mondays, Tuesdays, Wednesdays, and Thursdays. Learners attend either a morning or an afternoon session, keeping sensory demands manageable and predictable. Full days are not scheduled to prevent task fatigue.
