Educational Pathways & Daily Routine

Specialist, non-school vocational learning mapped directly to EHCP Preparation for Adulthood (PfA) targets and assessed through evidence portfolios rather than exam pressure.

Commissioned / Local Authority

Statutory EOTAS Pathways (Section 61)

Tailored daytime placements for young people where education in a conventional school environment is determined unsuitable by the local authority.

  • Funded via Local Authority SEND Teams or Parent-Held Personal Budgets.
  • Structured progress tracking linked to Section F outcomes.
  • Lead Educator representation and reports for Annual Reviews.
  • Strictly part-time: 1 to 2 days per week (4.5 to 9 hours weekly).
Community / Parent Direct

Elective Home Education (EHE) Sessions

A gentle, pressure-free creative community hub for home-educated young people needing peer co-regulation and hands-on digital skills.

  • Supportive micro-cohorts (maximum 6 learners per session).
  • Low-demand pacing honouring sensory regulation and anxiety profiles.
  • Flexible retained desk options and trial session opportunities.
  • Inclusive of all AQA Unit Award Scheme certificates.

The Maker Day Routine (09:30 – 14:00)

The day balances pastoral arrival check-ins, applied creative focus, and sensory decompression breaks. Provision runs Tuesday to Thursday across 38 term-time weeks.

Time Session Focus Pedagogical & Operational Detail
09:30 – 10:00 Arrival & Soft Landing Low-sensory check-in, visual schedule setup, tea/drink, emotional baseline check.
10:00 – 11:15 Workshop Block 1: Digital Design Canva layout, vector personalization, template sizing on individual workstations.
11:15 – 11:45 Sensory Break & Snack Quiet breakout snug, independent sensory rest, low-demand parallel chat.
11:45 – 12:45 Workshop Block 2: Physical Print Sublimation heat presses, product inspection, quality control, packaging dispatch.
12:45 – 13:30 Lunch & Decompression Quiet dining area, muted acoustics, free downtime with zero academic pressure.
13:30 – 14:00 Studio Wrap-Up & Portfolios Evidence logging for AQA portfolios, tool check-in, structured calm departure.
