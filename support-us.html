Why Tactile Crafts Matter

For a young person experiencing nervous system burnout or school-related anxiety, high-demand classroom tasks can feel completely overwhelming.

At Workshop House CIC, our Gentle Maker Station offers an immediate, soothing alternative: self-paced, tactile, and sensory-friendly making where there are no strict right-or-wrong answers and zero production targets.

Learners pick an activity card from our binder, grab the matching storage box, and settle into their own rhythm—whether that is rolling air-dry clay, winding yarn pom-poms, designing with cake decorating tools, or creating pipe cleaner botanical sculptures.

Physical Donations Welcome: Have unopened craft supplies, wool skeins, clay tools, or storage tubs looking for a good home? Get in touch to arrange a donation drop-off.

What We Are Sourcing

Every item is selected specifically for low-stakes, sensory-grounded creative exploration:

🏺 Clay & Modelling

Air-dry clay, terracotta, rolling pins, pottery sponge blocks, and wooden stylus tools.

🧵 Textiles & Fibers

Soft yarn skeins, loom bands, wooden peg dolls, safety sewing kits, and embroidery threads.

✂️ Paper & Construction

Heavyweight paper, origami sheets, safety contour scissors, glue sticks, pipe cleaners, and paper straws.

🎂 Cold Edible Crafts

Ready-to-roll modelling fondant, biscuit blanks, plunger cutters, edible ink markers, and texture mats.

🎨 Paints & Mark Making

Watercolour discs, soft-grip brushes, wax resist pastels, and washable clean-up supplies.

🗄️ Accessible Storage

Clear labelled storage bins, drawer carts, and heavy-duty binders with laminated visual guides.
