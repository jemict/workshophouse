Why Workshop House Matters

Across Worcestershire, some young people struggle to access mainstream education due to sensory overload, anxiety, and overwhelming demands. When education breaks down, isolation often follows.

Founded by a specialist teacher, Workshop House CIC offers a gentler, practical way forward. Rather than focusing solely on traditional academic outcomes, we provide small-group sessions and meaningful, hands-on activities in an environment that recognises different needs and ways of learning.

Our vision centres on two complementary creative areas: a Gentle Maker Station for accessible, self-paced crafts without pressure, and a Digital & Sublimation Station powered by our in-house brand, Dotty Moo, offering real-world enterprise and merchandise production skills.

Two Maker Stations. Real Skills.

We are developing two distinct areas to give young people different ways to engage, learn, and discover their strengths:

🏺 Gentle Maker: Clay & Textiles

Accessible, hands-on projects using air-dry clay, weaving, sewing, embroidery, and other practical, low-pressure sensory activities.

✂️ Gentle Maker: Papercraft & Art

Self-paced creative exploration through origami, painting, card-making, and structured projects using visual, step-by-step guides.

🎂 Gentle Maker: Creative Food

Practical decorating activities that encourage experimentation and self-expression through cold edible crafts and icing.

💻 Dotty Moo: Digital Design

Creating artwork and learning how designs are prepared for different products, helping young people see how ideas become reality.

🖨️ Dotty Moo: Sublimation

Exploring product personalisation, heat pressing, printing processes, and safe working practices to create custom merchandise like mugs.

📦 Dotty Moo: Enterprise Skills

Connecting creativity with real-world skills through quality checking, packaging, product photography, and order preparation.
