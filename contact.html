Gentle Admissions & Enquiries

We know exploring new environments after school-related distress takes immense courage. Our 4-step onboarding journey is unhurried, low-demand, and transparent.

01

Initial Conversation

An informal phone call or Teams chat with parents or caseworkers to discuss sensory profiles, interests, and needs.

02

Door-Open Visit

A quiet, zero-demand walk-through outside learner hours. The young person can look around, touch the machines, or just sit quietly.

03

Low-Demand Taster

A short practical trial making a personalized item (like a mug or print) alongside staff to assess nervous system comfort.

04

Agreed Placement

Confirming part-time schedule (1–2 sessions/week) via Local Authority EOTAS agreement or EHE community desk.
